About EverGreen

Growing friendships, celebrating culture, and enriching lives

The Central Florida EverGreen Seniors Club, established in 2016, is a nonprofit organization serving Chinese seniors aged 55 and above in the greater Orlando area. The club provides a welcoming and vibrant community through cultural celebrations, social gatherings, wellness activities, performing arts, and recreational programs.

With more than 100 members, EverGreen promotes friendship, active aging, cultural connection, and a strong sense of belonging. We strive to enrich the lives of our members by creating opportunities to stay active, build friendships, share experiences, and enjoy life together.

🌱

Our Mission

To bring members together through community activities, educational programs, social events, wellness programs, and volunteer opportunities, creating a welcoming environment where everyone can participate, contribute, and grow together.

❤️

Our Values

Friendship, respect, service, and growth guide our community. We value meaningful relationships, welcome different experiences and ideas, give back to our community, and continue learning and growing together.

Our History

2016

The Beginning

The Central Florida EverGreen Seniors Club was initiated by Ms. Wendi Chen, with Mr. Ping Qiao, Mr. Rongcai Wu, Ms. Xiaoying Huang, and others serving as founding members. On August 8, 2016, Ms. Chen officially submitted the organization's formation documents to the State of Florida.

2016

The Inaugural Meeting

In the early days, many generous supporters made donations and provided strong encouragement to the new organization. On August 12, 2016, EverGreen held its inaugural meeting at First Bank in Orlando. Mr. Ping Qiao was elected as the first president, Ms. Xiaoying Huang as vice president, and Mr. Rongcai Wu was responsible for financial affairs.

2016–18

Growing Together

In its early years, EverGreen regularly gathered in the activity room at First Bank. Members enjoyed singing, sharing food, and talking together in a warm and friendly atmosphere. With assistance from Mr. Shaoying Tian, the club successfully registered as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization and was approved to use the senior center facility on Marks Street.

2018+

A Home at Marks Street

Since 2018, EverGreen's regular activities have continued at the Marks Street senior center. Over the years, the club has grown into a vibrant community with more than 100 members.

Celebrating Traditions

EverGreen celebrates a wide variety of Chinese traditional festivals, including Chinese New Year, the Dragon Boat Festival, and the Mid-Autumn Festival. Living in the United States, we also embrace local traditions by celebrating holidays such as Halloween and Christmas together.

Food is an important part of our gatherings. Members often bring their favorite homemade dishes to share, while the club also orders delicious meals from local Chinese restaurants. During the Dragon Boat Festival, board members prepare zongzi by hand for our members, offering meat, vegetarian, and sweet varieties. At our Mid-Autumn Festival gatherings, mooncakes symbolize reunion and bring an especially warm feeling to the celebration.

Strong Leadership & Continuity

Mr. Rongcai Wu later served as the club's second president, with Ms. Chunying Zheng serving as vice president. Today, the club is led by Dr. Bing Zhang as president and Ms. Chunying Zheng as vice president.

Dr. Zhang has generously supported EverGreen and its development for many years. Ms. Zheng coordinates the club's day-to-day activities. In addition, EverGreen has seven board members who oversee areas including cultural programs, finance, logistics, membership, and community relations. Former president Mr. Rongcai Wu continues to serve as an advisor and contribute to the club's development.

🎭

Arts & Culture

Performing arts are an important part of EverGreen's community life. Members regularly practice traditional dance, fashion shows, and other performances. Some members have dance experience, while many discovered their love of dance after joining the club. Members encourage and learn from one another in a warm and supportive environment.

🎶

Choir & Performances

In 2025, EverGreen established a choir. Members practice Chinese and international choral music together, including rounds, two-part harmony, and multi-part singing. The club has also performed at Chinese New Year celebrations hosted by the Orlando Chinese Professional Association and has been invited to participate in events at Guang Ming Temple and Orange County multicultural celebrations.

🧘

Health & Wellness

EverGreen offers line dancing and Baduanjin exercises to support members' health, fitness, and interests. These activities provide opportunities to stay active while enjoying time with friends.

🎲

Hobbies & Social Groups

Chess, Mahjong, and other recreational activities are popular among members. The emphasis is not on winning or losing, but on social connection, mental exercise, and friendship. EverGreen also has interest groups for fishing, gardening, and other hobbies where members share experiences and enjoy their interests together.

A Warm Community

Many EverGreen members have entered retirement, and here they have found a sense of belonging, friendship, and companionship. Together, we rediscover the joy and meaning of life through shared experiences and meaningful activities.

We warmly welcome friends aged 55 and above to join our community, share life's wonderful moments, enjoy the years ahead, and keep life evergreen.

Written by the EverGreen Club Board of Directors
